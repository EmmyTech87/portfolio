About Me

My journey into web development actually started with a love for action games like Free Fire and Call of Duty — I wanted to build something just as engaging myself. What really kept me going, though, was Tony Stark. His inventions, especially AI assistants like J.A.R.V.I.S. and E.D.I.T.H., planted a real dream in me: to become a tech expert in my own way, building things that actually work, not just look cool on screen.

I'm fully self-taught — no physical tutor, just AI-guided courses, YouTube tutorials, and cheatsheets to help me get the hang of syntax, tags, and core concepts, then building real projects to lock it all in. JavaScript is my current challenge; it's a huge language, but I'm getting through it with help from resources like Claude. And this portfolio is proof I'm not just theorizing — I built an earlier version of this exact site, and what you're looking at now is me levelling that same project up.

Long-term, I want to grow into a full-stack developer with AI & ML skills in my toolkit — eventually building intelligent systems and my own personal AI assistant (yes, still chasing that Stark dream). Game development is on my radar too. For now, though, I'm focused on landing a solid, well-paying role or building a freelance career, while expanding past JavaScript into other languages that'll let me build full websites, apps, softwares, and games.
